VetAssistant Pro

An iPhone and iPad app for veterinary technicians. It records patient history and vitals by typing or by voice, and works out drug doses for boluses, CRIs and reversals. A working prototype is built. The mock on this page shows its screens.

What it does

History

Type or dictate the owner's history. Quick headings like Appetite and Vomiting keep notes consistent.

Vitals

Weight with automatic kg conversion, HR, RR and effort, CRT with mucous membrane color, and temperature (aural or rectal). Say them in one sentence and the app fills in the fields.

Calculations

Bolus volumes, CRIs by syringe pump or fluid bag, and reversals sized to the drug and strength given. Every result shows its math.

How it's built

PlatformNative iOS and iPadOS 18 or later, written in Swift and SwiftUI
Data storageSwiftData, saved only on the device. No server, no login, no sync.
VoiceApple's Speech framework. It's set to on-device recognition, so audio stays on the device on supported models.
Network useNone in the current prototype
PermissionsMicrophone and Speech Recognition, asked the first time someone taps Dictate
TestingAutomated tests cover the dose math and the voice-to-vitals parsing
Third-party codeNone. It uses only Apple frameworks.

Data and privacy

Patient and client details never leave the device in this version. Records are included in the device's own backup if backups are turned on. The app doesn't connect to our practice management software. Techs still chart there. This app is a working tool, not the medical record.

Next step: AI help with vitals

The next step is an AI service that turns loose dictation into structured vitals, for example "Bella's about sixty-two pounds, heart's at 96, pinkish, CRT under two." Today a built-in rule-based parser does this on the device. It works, but it needs fairly set phrasing.

On safety: the calculators show their work, the drug library's strengths can be edited to match what we stock, and the app reminds staff that every dose is confirmed with the attending veterinarian.
